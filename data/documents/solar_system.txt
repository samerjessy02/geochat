SOLAR SYSTEM FACT SHEET
========================

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star located at the center of the Solar System.
It accounts for about 99.86% of the total mass of the Solar System.

PLANETS (in order from the Sun):
1. Mercury - smallest planet, no moons, orbital period 88 days.
2. Venus - hottest planet, surface temperature around 465 degrees Celsius.
3. Earth - the only planet known to support life, has one moon.
4. Mars - the Red Planet, has two moons named Phobos and Deimos.
5. Jupiter - largest planet, has 95 known moons including Ganymede.
6. Saturn - famous for its ring system, has 146 known moons.
7. Uranus - an ice giant that rotates on its side.
8. Neptune - the windiest planet, with winds up to 2,100 km/h.

KEY FACTS
---------
- The asteroid belt lies between Mars and Jupiter.
- Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.
- Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.
- The Kuiper Belt is a region of icy bodies beyond Neptune.
- One astronomical unit (AU) is the average distance from Earth to the Sun,
  approximately 149.6 million kilometers.
