The Beginner's Guide to Brewing Coffee

Introduction

Coffee is one of the most popular beverages in the world, with over 2 billion cups consumed daily. The flavor of a cup depends on the bean origin, roast level, grind size, water temperature, and brew method.

Roast Levels

  • Light roast: higher acidity, more caffeine, bright fruity notes.
  • Medium roast: balanced flavor, the most common choice.
  • Dark roast: bold, smoky, lower acidity, less caffeine.

Brewing Ratios

A common starting ratio is 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water (often written as 1:16). For a stronger cup, use a 1:14 ratio.

The ideal water temperature for brewing is between 90 and 96 degrees Celsius (195 to 205 Fahrenheit).

Popular Methods

  1. French Press: steep for 4 minutes, then press.
  2. Pour Over: takes about 3 minutes, produces a clean cup.
  3. Espresso: 25 to 30 second extraction under 9 bars of pressure.
  4. Cold Brew: steep coarse grounds in cold water for 12 to 24 hours.

Storage Tip

Store beans in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for about 3 to 4 weeks after roasting.
