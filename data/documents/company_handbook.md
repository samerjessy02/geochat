# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## About Acme Corp

Acme Corp was founded in 2011 by Jane Doe and Rahul Mehta. The company
is headquartered in Austin, Texas, and builds cloud-based logistics software.
As of 2025, Acme employs 480 people across four offices.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, Monday through Friday.
Employees may work remotely up to **three days per week** with manager approval.

## Paid Time Off

| Tenure           | Annual PTO Days |
|------------------|-----------------|
| 0–2 years        | 15              |
| 3–5 years        | 20              |
| 6+ years         | 25              |

Unused PTO can be carried over, up to a maximum of **5 days** per year.

## Expense Reimbursement

- Submit expenses within **30 days** of purchase.
- Receipts are required for any expense over **$25**.
- Reimbursements are processed on the **15th of each month**.

## Security Policy

All employees must enable two-factor authentication on company accounts.
Passwords must be at least **12 characters** and rotated every 90 days.
Report suspected phishing to security@acmecorp.example.

## Contact

For HR questions, email hr@acmecorp.example or call extension 4400.
